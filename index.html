01 / Our approachHow we work

Start with
an actual problem.

Checking conditions before a boat trip can mean working through several sources. That takes time. Bringing the relevant information into one brief is a job software can help with.

That's how we approach a product: find a task people already do, take out some of the work, and keep setup to a minimum.

01

One job at a time

Pick a specific task. Keep the product small enough to do it well.

02

AI where it helps

Use AI to organize and summarize information. Build the interface around what people need to do.

03

Make it checkable

People should be able to see where information comes from and understand what a product can and can't do.
